ONE ARTIST TO BEGIN

A small studio with room to grow.

Limner begins as a direct conversation between you and one artist: choose a public-domain work, decide how much of it to make, and let the physical object take shape.

As the studio grows, other artists can join with their own materials, processes, and ways of seeing. Each artist will set their own price, with room for a conversation and negotiation.
