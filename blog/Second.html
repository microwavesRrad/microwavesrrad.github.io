Where Vonnegut Observed a Wyn

I am not a writer. I am not a reader. However, every now and then I read and write. Here, I am hoping to write down what I have read. I recently read Timequake by Vonnegut. Vonnegut is the best of company, especially for company keepers who keep losing what they had hoped to keep. I cannot remember what inspired me to read Timequake Sunday morning a few weeks back, but Vonnegut calls every now and again. I'd encourage you to pickup every Timequake.
